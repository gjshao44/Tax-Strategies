# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

### 1. Data Privacy by Design (Local Processing)
Your data privacy is treated with the highest priority. The Tax Strategies Application operates strictly on a **local, user-enclosed processing model**:

* **No Financial Data Collection:** Any personal financial variables, income brackets, or asset numbers you enter into the app are processed entirely within your machine's local execution environment or temporary browser session memory.
* **No Server Transmission:** Your data is never transmitted to, stored on, or accessed by G. Shao or any outside third-party servers.
* **No Persistence:** Once you terminate the application process or close your current browser session, all entered parameters are instantly and permanently erased from memory.

### 2. Third-Party Web Infrastructure
If you choose to run or access a version of this application hosted on a public web cloud service (such as Streamlit Community Cloud), that specific infrastructure platform may maintain standard network logs (such as IP addresses) strictly required to serve the web application interfaces. Please refer to your hosting provider's standalone privacy documentation for their infrastructure protocol.

### 3. Contact & Inquiries
For code-level questions regarding this application or its local execution layout, please open a formal issue tracking item directly inside the official project repository.
